About Me

Hi! I’m a frontend developer who is actively growing in the field of web technologies and programming. My main goal is to create high-quality, user-friendly, and unique websites for you, combining style, speed, and thoughtful design in every detail.

My programming journey started over a year ago, and since then I’ve completed more than 10 C# projects (including 3–4 successful commercial ones) and over 20 web projects, most of which reached their goals. Around 10–20% of them were experimental or educational, but each one helped me grow and deepen my technical knowledge.

In 2024, I made my first attempts at game development and continue exploring this field — especially through the Roblox platform, where I work on gameplay logic and mechanics using Lua. Besides that, I often work with C# and C++, expanding my skills in game development and software logic.

Most of my work revolves around websites — building interfaces and bringing creative ideas to life as complete products. I also offer website sales and can create small custom websites, from personal portfolios to simple online stores. All my code is written entirely by me, though I sometimes use online resources to learn and improve.

Beyond development, I create content on TikTok and plan to grow my presence on YouTube, where I’ll share my process of building websites, games, and my overall experience as a developer. For me, every new project isn’t just code — it’s a step forward that opens new opportunities.

I strive to grow as both a developer and a creator — combining design, logic, and creativity to make technology more accessible and engaging for you.
